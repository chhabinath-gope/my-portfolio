MY WORK

Featured Project

01

Customer Churn Prediction

An end-to-end Machine Learning web application that predicts whether a customer is likely to churn based on customer profile, services, contract and billing information.

The application provides a churn prediction, churn probability, customer risk classification and recommended action through an interactive Flask-based web interface.

73.9% Accuracy
78.3% Recall
84.2% ROC-AUC
Model Selection

Balanced Logistic Regression was selected because its higher recall helps identify more customers who may be at risk of churning.
